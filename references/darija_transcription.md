# Moroccan Darija (الدارجة المغربية) for Short-Form Video & Reels

## 1. The Core Challenge of Moroccan Darija in AI Pipelines

Moroccan Darija is a spoken dialect (vernacular) that differs significantly from Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى):
- **Phonetics & Morphology**: Heavy consonant clustering, vowelless prefixes, Berber (Amazigh) substratum, and loanwords from French, Spanish, and English.
- **Unstandardized Spelling**: Darija is written in two major formats on social media:
  1. **Arabizi / Darija Latin**: Written with the Latin alphabet using numbers for Arabic sounds that have no English equivalent:
     - `3` = ع (`'Ayn` e.g. *3endna*, *ma3louma*)
     - `7` = ح (`Haa` e.g. *7etta*, *7aja*, *rib7*)
     - `9` = ق (`Qaf` e.g. *9tissad*, *9olna*)
     - `5` = خ (`Kha` e.g. *5edma*, *5asna*)
  2. **Arabic Script (الدارجة بالحروف العربية)**: Written in Arabic script (e.g. *السلام الخوت، اليوم غانهضرو على...*).

## 2. Choosing Between Arabizi and Arabic Script for Reels

Audience / Account Style | Recommended Script | Why
:--- | :--- | :---
**Tech, AI, Crypto & Coding** | **Arabizi (Latin + Numbers)** | High engagement among Moroccan Gen-Z & tech community. Seamlessly blends with English terms (*AI, model, token, prompt, algorithm*).
**General Public, Broad Business** | **Arabic Script** | Higher accessibility across older demographics, North Africa, and Middle Eastern diaspora.

## 4. Best AI Models for Darija Speech-to-Text

1. **Google Gemini (2.0 Flash / 1.5 Pro)**:
   - Evaluated as the top performer for conversational Darija due to multimodal audio training on multilingual dialogues.
   - Accurately captures colloquial idioms, mixed French/English/Darija codeswitching, and generates clean Arabizi or Arabic text.
2. **Specialized Darija Whisper (HuggingFace)**:
   - Community fine-tuned models (e.g. `whisper-large-v3-darija`).
   - Standard baseline Whisper (`openai/whisper-large-v3`) can mistake Darija for MSA or Egyptian Arabic if not prompted with initial language context.
